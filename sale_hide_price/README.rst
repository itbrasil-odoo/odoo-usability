This module lets each quotation or sales order decide whether prices and
total amounts are visible on the customer portal and on the standard
sale PDF.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

Go to the sales order and open **Other Info**.

Use **Show Portal Values** to display line amounts on the portal and
PDF.

Use **Show Portal Total Amount** to display totals on the portal and
PDF.
